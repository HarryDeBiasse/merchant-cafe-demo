Our Story

A village cornerstone since the 1800s.

This corner of Pottersville Road began as the Lindabery "Blue Knot" general store — the heart of the village, where neighbors came to trade, gather, and catch up. By 1900 it was also the town post office, run by three generations of the same family.

A century later it was carefully preserved as a bank, its original character kept intact, and it still stands within the Pottersville Village Historic District. Now it begins its next chapter — as a coffee house.

The name is no accident: we're The Merchant, a nod to the general store that started it all. Come for the coffee — stay for the room that remembers.
